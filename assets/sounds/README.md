# Scanner Sound Files

Place your sound files here:

- `success.mp3` - Played when a ticket is successfully validated
- `error.mp3` - Played when a ticket is rejected

## Recommended Specifications

- Format: MP3
- Duration: 0.5 - 1 second
- Sample rate: 44.1 kHz
- Bitrate: 128 kbps
- Mono or stereo

## Free Sound Sources

- [Freesound.org](https://freesound.org/) - Search for "success", "error", "notification"
- [Zapsplat](https://www.zapsplat.com/) - Free with attribution
- [Mixkit](https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/) - Free for commercial use

## Example Sounds

**Success**: Short positive chime or "ding" sound
**Error**: Short buzz or "wrong" sound

## Fallback

If sound files are not present, the scanner will work without audio feedback.
